## About This Book

**Selling by Phone** is a comprehensive professional training manual designed to transform anyone into a highly effective phone sales professional. Whether you're making your first cold call or you're a seasoned closer looking to sharpen your skills, this book gives you the strategies, scripts, psychological frameworks, and real-world tools you need to succeed.

### What Makes This Book Different

- **Real product examples**: Every technique is demonstrated using **AgentBridge**, a real AI-powered office automation platform that you can sell to businesses today
- **No jargon**: Written in simple, accessible language for all experience levels
- **Actionable scripts**: Ready-to-use phone scripts for every stage of the sales conversation
- **Psychology-driven**: Deep understanding of buyer behavior, cognitive biases, and influence techniques
- **2026 updated**: Reflects the latest market trends, AI integration, and regulatory landscape
- **Historical context**: Anecdotes, historical events, and curiosities that make learning engaging

### Who This Book Is For

- 🎯 **New salespeople** entering the world of phone sales
- 📈 **Experienced sellers** wanting to upgrade their techniques with AI-powered tools
- 🏢 **Sales managers** building and training phone sales teams
- 🚀 **Entrepreneurs** who need to sell their own products by phone
- 💼 **Freelancers and consultants** looking to generate leads through phone outreach
- 🤖 **AgentBridge resellers** who want professional sales training for this specific product

### About AgentBridge

This book uses **AgentBridge** as the product being sold in all examples. AgentBridge is a self-hosted AI personal assistant that automates office work — from document creation to email management, from phone calls to data analysis. It runs on your own hardware, keeps all data private, and can replace repetitive office tasks with AI agents.
