# Telefonischer Customer Success

## 57.1 Onboarding
- Welcome call within 24 hours of purchase
- Set expectations for implementation timeline
- Assign a dedicated success contact
- Schedule training sessions
- Check in weekly during first month

## 57.2 Adoption
- Monitor usage patterns
- Call if adoption is low: "I noticed you haven't used [feature] yet. Can I help?"
- Share tips and best practices regularly
- Offer additional training when needed
- Celebrate adoption milestones

## 57.3 Support
- Responsive phone support (answer within 2 hours)
- Proactive check-ins (monthly for key accounts)
- Quick resolution of issues
- Follow-up after every support interaction
- Escalation path for complex issues

## 57.4 Renewals
- Start renewal conversation 90 days before expiry
- Review value delivered during the term
- Discuss expansion opportunities
- Offer loyalty pricing
- Make renewal easy (one call, one signature)

## 57.5 Upsell
- Identify expansion opportunities from usage data
- Time upsell after a success or milestone
- Present as natural extension of current value
- Quantify the additional ROI
- Make it easy to say yes

## 57.6 Feedback
- Regular satisfaction surveys (quarterly)
- NPS surveys (net promoter score)
- Feature request collection
- Churn interviews (why did they leave?)
- Act on feedback and communicate changes

## 57.7 Metrics
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| NPS | ≥ 50 |
| Renewal rate | ≥ 90% |
| Expansion rate | 20-30% |
| Time to value | < 30 days |
| Customer satisfaction | ≥ 4/5 |
| Churn rate | < 5% |

## 57.8 Best Practices
- Be proactive, not reactive
- Build relationships, not transactions
- Deliver value consistently
- Communicate regularly (not just when you want something)
- Make it easy to reach you
- Follow through on every promise

## Chapter Summary
Customer success on the phone is about proactive relationship management:
- Onboard thoroughly
- Drive adoption actively
- Support responsively
- Renew smoothly
- Expand naturally
- Listen continuously
