# Plan zur kontinuierlichen Verbesserung

## 39.1 Self-Analysis
Weekly self-review:
- What went well this week?
- What didn't?
- What did I learn?
- What will I do differently next week?

## 39.2 Recordings and Feedback
- Record your calls (with consent)
- Listen to 2-3 calls per week
- Identify patterns (good and bad)
- Share with your coach/manager for feedback

## 39.3 Coaching
- Weekly 1-on-1 coaching sessions
- Focus on specific skills, not general motivation
- Set one improvement goal per week
- Practice between sessions
- Review progress next session

## 39.4 Training
- Monthly training sessions (new techniques, product updates)
- Quarterly workshops (intensive skill-building)
- Annual conferences (industry trends, networking)
- Online learning (courses, books, podcasts)

## 39.5 SMART Goals
- **Specific**: "Improve my opening line connect rate from 15% to 25%"
- **Measurable**: Track connect rate daily
- **Achievable**: Based on benchmark data, this is realistic
- **Relevant**: Directly impacts my quota
- **Time-bound**: Achieve within 4 weeks

## 39.6 30-60-90 Day Plan
**Days 1-30**: Learn product, master basic scripts, make 30 calls/day
**Days 31-60**: Refine technique, improve qualification, hit 80% of target
**Days 61-90**: Full productivity, mentor newer sellers, exceed target

## 39.7 Certifications
- Internal product certification
- Sales methodology certification (SPIN, Challenger, Sandler)
- Compliance certification (GDPR, ROC)
- Communication skills certification

## 39.8 Community of Practice
- Weekly team huddles (share wins, learnings, challenges)
- Peer observation (listen to each other's calls)
- Shared knowledge base (tips, scripts, objections)
- Mentorship program (pair new sellers with experienced ones)

## 39.9 Innovation and Experimentation
- Test new approaches regularly
- A/B test scripts, timing, channels
- Share results with the team
- Adopt what works, discard what doesn't
- Stay curious about new tools and techniques

---

## Chapter Summary
Continuous improvement is the difference between good and great. Commit to:
- Weekly self-analysis
- Regular coaching and feedback
- Ongoing training and learning
- SMART goals with clear timelines
- Community and peer learning
- Experimentation and innovation
