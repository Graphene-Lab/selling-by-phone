# Planung von Telefonkampagnen

## 54.1 Objectives
Define clear campaign objectives:
- Generate X qualified leads
- Book Y demos
- Close Z deals
- Generate €X pipeline value
- Increase awareness of AgentBridge in target segment

## 54.2 Target
- Define ICP for the campaign
- Build target list (100-1000 prospects)
- Segment by industry, size, role, signal
- Prioritize by intent and fit score

## 54.3 Messages
- Core message aligned with campaign objective
- Personalized variations by segment
- A/B test different messages
- Ensure message consistency across channels
- Test before full deployment

## 54.4 Channels
- Primary: Phone (cold/warm calls)
- Supporting: Email, LinkedIn, SMS
- Sequence: Multi-touch across channels
- Timing: Coordinated across channels

## 54.5 Timing
- Campaign duration: 2-6 weeks typical
- Call timing: Optimal hours (10-12, 14-16)
- Frequency: 3-5 touches per prospect
- Follow-up cadence: Day 1, 3, 7, 14, 30

## 54.6 Budget
- List acquisition cost
- Tool costs (CRM, dialer, email platform)
- Personnel costs (SDR time)
- Content creation costs
- Total cost per acquisition target

## 54.7 Monitoring
- Daily: Calls made, connections, appointments
- Weekly: Pipeline generated, conversion rates
- Mid-campaign: Adjust based on early results
- End: Full performance report

## 54.8 Optimization
- A/B test openings, messages, timing
- Kill underperforming segments early
- Double down on what works
- Document learnings for next campaign
- Iterate and improve

---

## Chapter Summary
Phone campaigns require clear objectives, targeted lists, tested messages, coordinated channels, proper timing, budget planning, active monitoring, and continuous optimization.
