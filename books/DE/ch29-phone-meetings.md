# Telefonmeetings leiten

## 29.1 Preparation
Before any phone meeting: set an agenda, confirm attendees, test technology, prepare materials, and define desired outcomes.

## 29.2 Agenda
Every phone meeting needs a clear agenda shared in advance:
- Purpose of the meeting (1 line)
- Topics to cover (3-5 items max)
- Time allocated per topic
- Desired outcome
- Action items from previous meeting (if applicable)

## 29.3 Moderation
As the meeting moderator:
- Start on time (respect everyone's schedule)
- Stick to the agenda
- Manage dominant speakers gently
- Draw in quiet participants
- Keep discussions focused
- Summarize decisions and action items before ending

## 29.4 Participants
- Confirm all participants know the purpose and their role
- Identify the decision maker
- Ensure the right people are present (not too many, not too few)
- Have backup contacts if key people cannot attend

## 29.5 Documentation
- Take notes during the meeting (or assign someone)
- Record if all parties consent
- Document decisions, action items, owners, and deadlines
- Share notes within 24 hours

## 29.6 Action Items
End every meeting with clear action items:
- What needs to be done
- Who is responsible
- When it is due
- How progress will be tracked

## 29.7 Follow-Up
- Send meeting notes within 24 hours
- Confirm all action items in writing
- Schedule next meeting if needed
- Follow up on action items before the next meeting

## 29.8 Hybrid Meetings
For meetings with some participants on phone and others in person:
- Ensure phone participants can hear and be heard clearly
- Use speakerphone or conferencing tools
- Include phone participants in visual discussions verbally
- Send materials in advance so everyone can follow along

---

## Chapter Summary
Phone meetings require the same discipline as in-person meetings: preparation, agenda, moderation, documentation, and follow-up. The lack of visual cues means you must be MORE structured, not less.
