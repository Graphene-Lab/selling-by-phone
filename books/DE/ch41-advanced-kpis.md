# Erweiterte KPIs und Benchmarks

## 41.1-41.8 Advanced Metrics
| Metric | Formula | Benchmark |
|--------|---------|-----------|
| Conversion by phase | Won/Entered per stage | Varies by stage |
| Pipeline velocity | (Deals × Win Rate × Deal Value) / Cycle Length | Higher = better |
| Average deal size | Total revenue / Deals closed | Track trend |
| Sales cycle length | Days from first contact to close | 2-6 weeks (B2B) |
| Churn rate | Lost customers / Total customers | < 5% annually |
| Lifetime value (LTV) | Avg deal × Avg years × Margin | 3-5x CAC |
| CAC:LTV ratio | CAC / LTV | < 1:3 |
| Net Revenue Retention | (Renewals + Expansion - Churn) / Starting ARR | > 100% |

## 41.10 Opportunity by Phase
| Phase | Entry Criteria | Exit Criteria |
|-------|---------------|---------------|
