# Check-list operative

## 63.1 Pre-Call Checklist
- [ ] Research completed (company, contact, signal)
- [ ] Objective defined (one clear goal)
- [ ] Script/notes prepared
- [ ] CRM record open
- [ ] Phone/headset tested
- [ ] Quiet environment confirmed
- [ ] Compliance checked (ROC, calling hours)
- [ ] Water nearby
- [ ] Notifications silenced
- [ ] Positive mindset (power pose, breathing)

## 63.2 During-Call Checklist
- [ ] Clear introduction (name + company)
- [ ] Pattern interrupt used
- [ ] Reason for call stated
- [ ] Permission obtained
- [ ] Discovery questions asked
- [ ] Listening > talking (60/40 ratio)
- [ ] Notes taken
- [ ] Objections addressed
- [ ] Value presented
- [ ] Next step requested

## 63.3 Post-Call Checklist
- [ ] CRM updated within 30 seconds
- [ ] Outcome logged
- [ ] Next step scheduled with date
- [ ] Follow-up task created
- [ ] Confirmation email sent (if applicable)
- [ ] Self-assessment (what went well? what to improve?)

## 63.4 Qualification Checklist
- [ ] ICP fit confirmed
- [ ] Pain identified and quantified
- [ ] Budget confirmed or estimated
- [ ] Decision maker identified
- [ ] Decision process understood
- [ ] Timeline established
- [ ] Champion identified (or plan to create one)

## 63.5 Closing Checklist
- [ ] All objections addressed
- [ ] Value clearly communicated
- [ ] ROI quantified
- [ ] Next step clearly defined
- [ ] Verbal agreement obtained
- [ ] Confirmation in writing sent
- [ ] Calendar invite sent (if meeting)

## 63.6 Follow-Up Checklist
- [ ] Follow-up email sent within 24 hours
- [ ] Next call scheduled
- [ ] Materials promised delivered
- [ ] CRM updated with follow-up date
- [ ] Reminder set
- [ ] Value-add content included in follow-up

## 63.7 Compliance Checklist
- [ ] ROC checked before calling
- [ ] Calling hours respected (9:00-20:00 weekdays)
- [ ] Self-identification clear
- [ ] Purpose of call stated
- [ ] Recording consent obtained (if recording)
- [ ] Opt-out honored immediately
- [ ] Data stored securely
- [ ] Retention policy followed

## 63.8 Coaching Checklist
- [ ] Call recording reviewed
- [ ] Specific behaviors identified (good and bad)
- [ ] Feedback prepared (specific, behavioral, actionable)
- [ ] Coaching session scheduled (same day if possible)
- [ ] Improvement goal set
- [ ] Practice exercise assigned
- [ ] Follow-up scheduled to check progress

---

## Chapter Summary
Checklists ensure consistency and prevent mistakes. Use them for every call, every process, every interaction. They are your safety net.

## Action Items
- [ ] Print these checklists and keep them at your desk
- [ ] Customize them for your specific process
- [ ] Use them in every call block
- [ ] Review and update monthly
- [ ] Share with your team for consistency
