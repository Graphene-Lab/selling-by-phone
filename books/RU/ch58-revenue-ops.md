# Revenue operations

## 58.1 Sales-Marketing Alignment
- Shared definitions (what is a qualified lead?)
- Shared goals (pipeline, revenue)
- Regular sync meetings (weekly)
- Shared CRM and data
- Closed-loop feedback (sales tells marketing what's working)

## 58.2 Data
- Single source of truth (CRM)
- Data quality standards
- Enrichment and hygiene processes
- Analytics and reporting
- Data governance policies

## 58.3 Process
- Defined sales process with clear stages
- Handoff protocols (marketing → sales → success)
- SLA for lead response time
- Process documentation
- Continuous improvement cycle

## 58.4 Technology
- CRM as the central hub
- Sales engagement platform
- Data enrichment tools
- Analytics and reporting
- AI-powered insights (AgentBridge integration)

## 58.5 Metrics
| Metric | Owner | Target |
|--------|-------|--------|
| Lead-to-opportunity rate | Marketing + Sales | 20-30% |
| Pipeline velocity | Sales | Track trend |
| Revenue per lead | Marketing | Track trend |
| CAC | Finance | < 1/3 LTV |
| Pipeline coverage | Sales | 3x quota |

## 58.6 Governance
- Data ownership and access policies
- Process compliance monitoring
- Regular process reviews
- Change management for process updates
- Compliance with regulations

## 58.7 Automation
- Lead routing automation
- Follow-up task automation
- Reporting automation
- Data enrichment automation
- Alert and notification automation

## 58.8 Continuous Improvement
- Monthly RevOps review
- Quarterly process optimization
- Annual technology audit
- Continuous feedback loops
- Benchmark against industry best practices

---

## Chapter Summary
Revenue Operations aligns sales, marketing, and customer success around shared data, processes, and goals. It eliminates silos and creates a unified revenue engine.
