# Chapter 38: Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

## 38.1 Talking Too Much
The #1 mistake. If you are talking more than 40% of the call, you are losing. Ask more questions. Listen more.

## 38.2 Rigid Script
Reading a script word-for-word sounds robotic. Use scripts as guides, not recitations. Be natural.

## 38.3 Failed Qualification
Not qualifying before investing time leads to wasted calls on unqualified prospects. Always check: fit, budget, authority, need, timeline.

## 38.4 Ignored Objections
Brushing off objections makes the prospect feel unheard. Address every objection with acknowledgment and a response.

## 38.5 Missing Follow-Up
80% of sales require 5+ touches. Most sellers give up after 1. The money is in the follow-up.

## 38.6 Outdated Data
Calling wrong numbers, wrong people, wrong companies wastes time and damages credibility. Keep your data fresh.

## 38.7 Burnout
Phone selling is emotionally demanding. Without proper breaks, mindset management, and support, burnout is inevitable.

## 38.8 Compliance Errors
Calling people on the do-not-call list, calling outside allowed hours, failing to identify yourself — these are legal violations with real consequences.

## 38.9 Measurement Errors
Not tracking metrics, tracking the wrong metrics, or misinterpreting data leads to poor decisions. Track the right things, accurately.

---

## Chapter Summary
Avoid these 9 common mistakes:
1. Talk too much → Listen more
2. Rigid script → Be natural
3. No qualification → Qualify first
4. Ignored objections → Address them
5. No follow-up → 5+ touches
6. Outdated data → Keep it fresh
7. Burnout → Manage energy
8. Compliance errors → Follow the law
9. Bad measurement → Track what matters
