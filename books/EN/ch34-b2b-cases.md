# Chapter 34: B2B Practical Cases

## 34.1 Lead Generation
B2B lead generation for AgentBridge combines multiple channels:
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator for prospecting
- Job boards for signal detection (admin job postings)
- Website analytics for inbound leads
- Content marketing for nurturing
- Referrals from existing customers

## 34.2 Qualification
Use MEDDIC for B2B qualification (see Chapter 13). Key questions:
- Company size and admin team size
- Current tools and pain points
- Budget and decision process
- Timeline and urgency
- Data privacy requirements

## 34.3 Appointments
B2B appointments require more preparation than B2C:
- Research the company thoroughly before calling
- Identify the decision maker and influencers
- Prepare a personalized reason for the call
- Offer specific times with clear agenda
- Confirm in writing with a meeting brief

## 34.4 Long Cycles
B2B sales cycles for AgentBridge typically run 2-6 weeks:
- Week 1: Initial contact and qualification
- Week 2: Demo
- Week 3: Internal review by prospect
- Week 4: Proposal and negotiation
- Week 5-6: Close and onboarding

**Managing long cycles:**
- Set clear next steps after every interaction
- Follow up consistently (3-5 touches minimum)
- Provide value at every touch (content, insights, answers)
- Build multiple relationships within the account
- Create urgency through cost-of-inaction calculations

## 34.5 Multi-Stakeholder
B2B deals involve multiple stakeholders:
- **End users** (admin staff): Care about ease of use
- **Manager** (COO): Care about efficiency
- **Finance** (CFO): Care about ROI
- **IT**: Care about security and integration
- **CEO**: Care about strategic advantage

**Strategy**: Address each stakeholder's concerns individually. Create tailored materials for each. Get your champion to advocate internally.

## 34.6 B2B Scripts
📞 **AgentBridge B2B cold call:**
"Hi Marco, this is Andrea from Graphene Lab. I noticed [Company] posted two admin positions last week. I'm calling because we help companies like yours automate 80% of admin work with AI — so you can grow without proportionally growing your admin team. Do you have 5 minutes to explore whether this could work for you?"

## 34.7 Sectors: SaaS, Manufacturing, Services
**SaaS**: Focus on automation, scalability, integration
**Manufacturing**: Focus on back-office efficiency, document automation
**Professional Services**: Focus on document creation, report generation, client deliverables

## 34.8 B2B Metrics
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Connect rate | 15-25% |
| Qualification rate | 20-30% of connected |
| Demo-to-proposal | 50-70% |
| Proposal-to-close | 40-60% |
| Average deal size | €3,000-15,000 |
| Sales cycle | 2-6 weeks |
