# Vente telephonique internationale et interculturelle

## 46.1 Cultural Differences
| Culture | Communication Style | Best Approach |
|---------|-------------------|---------------|
| **German** | Direct, formal, data-driven | Be precise, punctual, provide evidence |
| **French** | Formal, relationship-focused | Build rapport first, be patient |
| **American** | Direct, enthusiastic, fast | Match energy, get to the point |
| **Japanese** | Indirect, formal, consensus-based | Be very formal, allow time for decisions |
| **Italian** | Warm, expressive, relationship-driven | Build personal connection, be warm |
| **Russian** | Direct, skeptical, formal | Be prepared, show expertise, be patient |

## 46.2 Time Zones
- Schedule calls during their business hours, not yours
- Use a world clock tool
- Be aware of local holidays
- Rotate call times to accommodate different regions
- Consider hiring local sellers for key markets

## 46.3 Language and Accents
- Speak clearly and slightly slower for non-native speakers
- Avoid idioms, slang, and cultural references
- Use simple, universal language
- Consider hiring native speakers for key markets
- AgentBridge supports 6 languages (EN, IT, FR, ES, DE, RU)

## 46.4 Local Regulations
Each country has different telemarketing rules:
- Research local regulations before calling
- Check do-not-call registries
- Understand consent requirements
- Comply with local calling hour restrictions
- When in doubt, consult local legal counsel

## 46.5 Script Adaptation
- Translate scripts, but also culturally adapt them
- Adjust formality level (tu vs. vous, du vs. Sie)
- Modify examples to be locally relevant
- Use local currency and references
- Test with native speakers before deploying

## 46.6 Currency and Pricing
- Display prices in local currency
- Understand purchasing power differences
- Adjust pricing strategy by market
- Consider local payment methods
- Be aware of tax implications (VAT, GST)

## 46.7 Best Practices
- Research the culture before calling
- Learn basic greetings in their language
- Be respectful of cultural norms
- Adapt your style to their communication preferences
- Build local partnerships for market entry
- Be patient with longer decision cycles in some cultures

---

## Chapter Summary
International phone selling requires cultural awareness, language adaptation, regulatory compliance, and patience. What works in one country may fail in another. Research, adapt, and respect local norms.
