# Account-based selling telephonique

## 52.1 Account Selection
- Identify high-value accounts matching your ICP
- Prioritize by potential revenue, strategic fit, and buying signals
- Focus on quality over quantity (10 great accounts > 100 random prospects)
- Use data enrichment and intent signals for selection

## 52.2 Research
Deep account research before any call:
- Company: size, revenue, growth, strategy, challenges
- Industry: trends, competitive landscape, regulatory environment
- Stakeholders: roles, backgrounds, communication preferences
- Technology: current stack, gaps, recent changes
- Timing: budget cycles, strategic initiatives, recent news

## 52.3 Stakeholder Mapping
| Role | Influence | Concern | Approach |
|------|-----------|---------|----------|
| **CEO** | High | Strategy, growth | Vision, competitive advantage |
| **CFO** | High | Cost, ROI | Numbers, payback period |
| **COO** | High | Efficiency, capacity | Process improvement |
| **IT** | Medium | Security, integration | Technical specs, control |
| **End Users** | Low-Medium | Ease of use | Demo, training, support |

## 52.4 Personalized Messages
- Every message tailored to the specific account and stakeholder
- Reference specific company news, challenges, or initiatives
- Use their industry language and context
- Show you understand their specific situation
- Make every interaction feel bespoke

## 52.5 Team Coordination
- SDR identifies and warms up the account
- AE handles the demo and negotiation
- Customer Success ensures onboarding and expansion
- All team members share context through CRM
- Regular account team meetings (weekly for key accounts)

## 52.6 Metrics
| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Account penetration | 3+ stakeholders engaged |
| Deal size | 2-3x average |
| Sales cycle | Longer but higher value |
| Win rate | 30-50% (focused effort) |
| Expansion revenue | 20-30% of accounts expand |

## 52.7 Tools
- CRM with account hierarchy
- Intent data providers (6sense, Bombora)
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator
- Account research tools (ZoomInfo, Clearbit)
- AgentBridge for automated research and content generation

## 52.8 Case Example
🤖 **AgentBridge ABS Example:**
Target: 100-person professional services firm in Milan.
- Week 1: Research company, map stakeholders, identify signals (hiring admin staff)
- Week 2: SDR calls COO with personalized message referencing their growth
- Week 3: Demo for COO + IT Manager (addressing efficiency + security)
- Week 4: CFO briefing with ROI one-pager
- Week 5: Proposal and negotiation
- Week 6: Close and onboarding plan
- Month 3: Expansion discussion (additional modules)

## Chapter Summary
Account-based selling focuses your effort on high-value accounts with personalized, multi-stakeholder engagement. The phone is your primary tool for building relationships within the account.
