## 67.1 Education

## 67.2 Experience
Andrea is the founder of **Graphene Lab**, the company behind **AgentBridge** — a self-hosted AI personal assistant that automates office work while maintaining complete data privacy. With years of experience in enterprise software development and AI technology, Andrea has built a product that addresses real business needs: reducing costs, increasing productivity, and protecting sensitive data.

His experience spans:
- **AI and software development**: Building the AIOrchestrator library and AgentBridge platform
- **Enterprise automation**: Designing systems that replace repetitive office tasks with AI
- **Sales and business development**: Selling AI-powered solutions to businesses of all sizes
- **Open-source community**: Leading an AGPL v3.0 open-source project with global contributors
- **Product strategy**: Positioning self-hosted AI in a cloud-dominated market

## 67.5 Philosophy
Andrea believes that AI should serve humans, not replace them. AgentBridge embodies this philosophy: it automates the drudgery so people can focus on meaningful work. It keeps data private because privacy is a fundamental right. It is open-source because knowledge should be shared. And it is sold by phone because human connection still matters in a digital world.

"This book exists because I believe that selling a product you genuinely believe in should be easy. AgentBridge helps businesses. The techniques in this book help you communicate that help effectively. Together, they create a powerful combination: a great product sold by well-trained, ethical, passionate sellers."
