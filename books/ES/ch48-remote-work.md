# Trabajo remoto y productividad

## 48.1 Work Environment
- Dedicated workspace (even if small)
- Good lighting, comfortable chair, proper desk
- Noise-free or noise-canceling headphones
- Professional background for video calls
- All equipment within reach (phone, headset, water, notes)

## 48.2 Time Management
- Stick to a schedule (start time, call blocks, breaks, end time)
- Use time-blocking for different activities
- Set boundaries with household members
- Take regular breaks (5 min every hour)
- End the day at a consistent time

## 48.3 Distractions
- Turn off non-essential notifications
- Use "Do Not Disturb" during call blocks
- Keep phone on silent (except for calls)
- Close unnecessary browser tabs
- Use website blockers during focus time

## 48.4 Tools
- **CRM**: Cloud-based (accessible from anywhere)
- **Phone**: VoIP (softphone) with headset
- **Communication**: Slack/Teams for team interaction
- **Scheduling**: Calendly or CRM calendar
- **AI**: AgentBridge for research and automation

## 48.5 Internal Communication
- Daily standup (video or chat)
- Weekly team meeting
- Shared channels for wins, questions, and support
- Regular check-ins with manager
- Over-communicate rather than under-communicate

## 48.6 Wellbeing
- Take breaks away from the screen
- Exercise regularly
- Maintain social connections
- Set boundaries (work does not bleed into personal time)
- Practice stress management techniques

## 48.7 Performance
- Remote sellers can be as productive as office-based sellers
- Key: structure, discipline, and proper tools
- Monitor output (not hours)
- Trust but verify (metrics, not micromanagement)
- Provide support and coaching remotely

## 48.8 Best Practices
- Treat your home office like a real office
- Get dressed for work (psychological shift)
- Maintain the same routine as office-based sellers
- Communicate proactively with your team
- Invest in your setup (good headset, chair, desk)

---

## Chapter Summary
Remote phone selling works when you have:
- Proper environment and equipment
- Structured schedule and time management
- Minimal distractions
- Strong communication with your team
- Self-discipline and self-care
