# Gestion del tiempo y del CRM

## 6.1 Planning the Day

A phone seller's day lives or dies by how well it is planned. Without structure, you will waste hours between calls, lose track of follow-ups, and end the day feeling busy but unproductive.

**The ideal phone seller's day:**

| Time | Activity | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|
| 8:00-8:30 | Review pipeline, plan calls, research prospects | Preparation |
| 8:30-9:00 | Vocal warm-up, mental preparation | Readiness |
| 9:00-11:00 | **Call Block 1** (peak energy, best connect rate) | Primary selling |
| 11:00-11:30 | Break, notes, CRM updates | Recovery |
| 11:30-13:00 | **Call Block 2** (second wind) | Secondary selling |
| 13:00-14:00 | Lunch, complete disconnect | Recovery |
| 14:00-15:30 | **Call Block 3** (afternoon calls) | Follow-ups, warm calls |
| 15:30-16:00 | Break, email responses | Admin |
| 16:00-17:00 | Demos, meetings, complex calls | High-value activities |
| 17:00-17:30 | Day review, tomorrow's prep, CRM cleanup | Wrap-up |

💡 **Tip**: Protect your call blocks like sacred time. No meetings, no emails, no "quick questions" during call blocks. This is when you make money. Everything else can wait.

## 6.2 Call Blocks

Call blocks are dedicated periods of time where you make back-to-back calls without interruption. This is the most efficient way to sell by phone.

**Why call blocks work:**
- **Momentum**: Each call builds energy for the next. The first call is hardest; by call 5, you are in flow.
- **Focus**: You stay in "selling mode" instead of context-switching between tasks.
- **Efficiency**: No time wasted "getting ready" between calls.
- **Resilience**: A rejection in a call block is cushioned by the next call coming immediately.

**How to structure a call block:**
1. **Prepare 10-15 prospects** before the block starts (research done, notes ready)
2. **Set a timer** (90 minutes is ideal — long enough for momentum, short enough to maintain focus)
3. **Call back-to-back** — no breaks between calls within the block
4. **Take notes immediately** after each call (30 seconds max)
5. **Move to the next number** regardless of the outcome of the previous call
6. **End the block** when the timer goes off, even if you are "on a roll"

🤖 **AgentBridge Example**: A typical 90-minute call block for AgentBridge:
- 12-15 prospects prepared with research notes
- Mix of cold calls (new prospects) and warm calls (follow-ups)
- Target: 8-10 connections, 2-3 demos booked
- After the block: 15 minutes to update CRM, send follow-up emails, prepare for next block

## 6.3 Priorities and Pipeline

Not all calls are equal. Prioritize based on:

1. **Hot leads** (showed buying signals, requested info) → Call first
2. **Warm follow-ups** (previous conversation, promised to call back) → Call second
3. **Signal-based cold calls** (identified buying signal) → Call third
4. **General cold calls** (ICP fit but no specific signal) → Call last

**Pipeline priority matrix:**

| | High Urgency | Low Urgency |
|---|---|---|
| **High Value** | 🔴 Call NOW | 🟡 Schedule today |
| **Low Value** | 🟠 Call this block | 🟢 Call when time allows |

## 6.4 Data Entry

CRM data entry is not optional. It is the backbone of your sales process. If it is not in the CRM, it did not happen.

**Data entry best practices:**
- **Immediate**: Enter data within 30 seconds of the call ending. Do not batch it for later.
- **Complete**: Fill in all required fields. Partial data is useless.
- **Accurate**: Double-check names, numbers, and dates.
- **Actionable**: Every entry should include a next step with a date.
- **Consistent**: Use the same format and terminology every time.

⚠️ **Warning**: The most common CRM mistake is "I will enter it later." You will not. Or you will forget key details. Or you will enter it wrong because you are rushing at the end of the day. Enter it NOW, while the conversation is fresh.

## 6.5 Automations and Reminders

Modern CRMs offer automation features that save hours of manual work:

**Essential automations:**
- **Follow-up reminders**: Auto-create a task 2 days after a call if no next step is scheduled
- **Email sequences**: Auto-send a follow-up email after a demo booking
- **Stage change alerts**: Notify your manager when a deal moves to "proposal" stage
- **Task escalation**: If a follow-up task is overdue by 24 hours, escalate to your team lead
- **Data enrichment**: Auto-populate company data from LinkedIn when you create a new contact

The agent does this automatically, saving you 15-20 minutes of manual work every day.

## 6.6 Dashboards and Reports

You need visibility into your performance. A good dashboard shows:

**Daily dashboard:**
- Calls made / Calls connected / Connect rate
- Demos booked / Meetings held
- Pipeline value added
- Tasks completed / Tasks overdue

**Weekly dashboard:**
- Total calls and connections
- Conversion rates at each stage
- Revenue generated
- Activity vs. target
- Top performing scripts/approaches

**Monthly dashboard:**
- Pipeline health (distribution across stages)
- Win rate trends
- Average deal size trends
- Sales cycle length
- Forecast accuracy

## 6.8 Data Quality and CRM Hygiene

A CRM with bad data is worse than no CRM at all. It gives you false confidence, missed opportunities, and wasted time calling wrong numbers.

**CRM hygiene rules:**
1. **Deduplicate**: Check for existing records before creating new ones
2. **Verify**: Confirm email addresses and phone numbers are valid
3. **Update**: Change job titles, companies, and roles as people move
4. **Archive**: Move dead leads out of your active pipeline
5. **Standardize**: Use consistent naming conventions, stage names, and tags
6. **Clean regularly**: Schedule a weekly 30-minute CRM cleanup session

📊 **Data Point**: According to HubSpot, the average CRM loses 30% of its data accuracy every year due to people changing jobs, companies merging, and contact information becoming outdated. Regular cleaning is not optional — it is essential.
